# Disclaimer and community rules

Free Domain Name is an **experiment and demonstration**, not a domain registrar,
hosting provider, certificate authority, or content platform. The registry only
stores a small mapping: a name, an HTTP(S) origin, a GitHub handle, and a cache
TTL.

## Names and destinations are third-party content

- Every name and destination is submitted by a member of the public. The
  maintainers do not operate, control, monitor, or endorse the destination.
- A record in this repository is **not** a verification of ownership, identity,
  safety, legality, or quality.
- The destination may change, disappear, serve malware, phishing, or unlawful
  content, or collect data. **Visit third-party destinations at your own risk.**
- The browser displays the destination in an iframe and strips some framing and
  security headers so registry sites can render inside it. That is a deliberate
  trade-off, not a safety guarantee. See the browser's security notes.
- We do not provide uptime, availability, or fitness guarantees. The registry,
  the GitHub CDN, the browser, and any destination can fail or be removed at any
  time.

## Community reviews and scores

Community review threads live in this repository's GitHub Discussions. Each
discussion is tied to one registry name and is embedded in the browser's Stumble
page with giscus.

- A 👍/👎 reaction is a single person's opinion, not a security audit, rating
  agency, or endorsement by the project.
- Scores are the share of positive votes among recorded votes. They can be
  incomplete, stale, manipulated, or unrepresentative. Always read the comments
  and make your own judgment.
- GitHub comments are public and are subject to GitHub's terms and moderation.
  The maintainers may hide, lock, or delete comments and discussions, and may
  remove a registry record, at their discretion.
- **Human-written only.** Do not submit AI-generated domain lists, AI-generated
  comments, automated reviews, spam, or bulk promotional text. Maintainers may
  remove submissions or comments that appear automated, abusive, or off-topic.
  We cannot technically prove who wrote a comment; this is a community rule and
  a moderation policy, not a cryptographic guarantee.

## Reporting abuse

Open an issue in this repository with the title `Abuse report: <domain>` and
include the domain, the destination, and what is wrong. You can also report the
discussion comment directly to GitHub. For a security problem in
the browser itself, use the browser repository's security guidance. Do not post
secrets or personal data in a public issue.
